# Timeline

Read this before you change `src/ui/timeline.js`, `src/ui/drag.js` or the timeline styles.

`layout()` computes columns from `tlAvail()` (content width of `#main` minus card border and label column `LABEL`: 200 px, 168 px on the icon-bar layout). `T.fine` is true for one and two weeks: day headers, a click on a day creates a todo (six weeks: a topic). One week = 7 columns, two weeks = 14: weekdays wide, Saturday and Sunday 48 px (`WEEKEND`) unless today or with open todos due. Below 140 px the header shows "Mo" instead of "Montag"; in two weeks Mondays carry the week number from 110 px. Six weeks = 42 equal columns. Paging: one and two weeks move by one week, six weeks by two. Always position via `colOf()`, `pos()` and `dayAt()`.

Open todos sit under the topic bars on their due day in every view; overdue ones move to today's column while it is visible. The column width decides the form, not the view: from `CHIP_MIN` (100 px) cards with title, below that one round marker per person and day (`markHTML()`): one todo shows its look (team outlined, mine coral, waiting grey, overdue red with "!"), several show their number in the look of the most urgent one. Hovering or focusing a card or marker shows a hover card with the full title, kind, due date and the start of the note in every view (`#tlPop`, `position:fixed` because the timeline clips). It opens after `HOVER_MS` (150 ms) below a marker and after `HOVER_CHIP_MS` (350 ms) beside a card, so the cards below stay visible and moving across the board stays calm. Cards carry no `title` attribute, which would show a second tooltip. A single-todo marker drags and opens like a card; a click on a marker with several pins the card, its entries open the todo. On a 27" monitor two weeks show cards, on the 13" laptop markers. Topics are greedily placed on lanes (`placeLanes()`).

**Involved lane.** Topics of others in which the person is involved (`with`, `involvedTopics()`) show below the person's own bars as thin dashed bars (`wbarHTML()`, `WLANE`), labelled with the responsible person ("Lena: Payment-API v2"). They are buttons with `data-action="edit-topic"`: a click opens the topic, they never drag or resize, and drag-to-create and the click on a day ignore them (`.wbar` in `drag.js` and `events.js`). The responsible person keeps the real bar. The phone team grid (`gridRows()`) shows the same lane. Todos are not mirrored.

Pointer interactions (`drag.js`) share one `drag` object with a `type`: `bar` (move, or resize via `.h-l`/`.h-r`), `todo` (drop on a day and person via `elementFromPoint`), `create` (drag on empty area, then `inlineCreate()`). The inline field (`#tlNew`) creates a topic from plain text; an absence tag makes an absence over the dragged range, `#ich`/`#meins`/`#wartet` or a repeat tag a todo due on the last dragged day (`inlineParse()`, which uses `parseQuick()` once a tag is present). The hint below the field (`inlineHint()`) says what Enter will create. It offers `#` suggestions only; their list is `position:fixed` (`.sug-float`) because the timeline clips. After a drag, `suppressUntil` prevents the following click from opening a form; new pointer logic must do the same. On touch `create` does not start, so swiping scrolls. The phone has no timeline; it has the team grid below.

## Phone team grid

`views-phone.js` draws its own grid instead of the timeline: one row per person (me first), one column per workday. Weekends are left out, holidays are grey columns, absences hatched with their kind (a half day over half the column, morning left, afternoon right; label only in the day list), involved topics as the dashed lane. Positions are column indexes (`colStart()`/`colEnd()` move a weekend start to Monday and a weekend end to Friday, `colDay()` goes back), the column width is the CSS variable `--dw` from a container query (`100cqw`), so nothing is measured in JavaScript. A bar that runs past the grid has a square end (`cut-l`/`cut-r`).

- **Upright** (`viewTeam()`): one week (`ui.toff` weeks from now, arrows), a tap on a day header sets `ui.tday` and `dayList()` lists that day below: project, topics with their end, involvement, absence, todos due, overdue only for today. Absent people come last.
- **Sideways** (`lay-land`, `teamLand()`): `TEAM_BACK` weeks back to `TEAM_AHEAD` weeks ahead in a horizontal scroller (`.wg-land`), ten columns visible, free native scrolling without snapping. Week labels and bar titles are `position:sticky`, so they stay at the left edge; bars use `overflow:clip`, because `overflow:hidden` would make them the sticky container. The tab bar is hidden there (`body:has(.wg-land)`). `render()` restores the scroll position from `ui.lgX` (`restoreGridScroll()`); a route change starts again at the current week. Lanes are placed over all weeks, so a row can have an empty lane in some weeks.
- **Moving** (`drag.js`, type `grid`): a bar has to be held `GRID_HOLD` (450 ms) before it lifts; moving earlier is a scroll and cancels, a short tap is a click and opens the topic. Lifted, start and end shift by the workdays the finger moved (`wdShift()`), so a topic never lands on a weekend. The bar shows the new dates, near the edge of the sideways grid it scrolls along, on release `save()`, `render()` and a toast with undo. A non-passive `touchmove` handler stops scrolling while a bar is lifted, `contextmenu` is suppressed on bars, `suppressUntil` swallows the click afterwards. No resizing and no drag-to-create on the phone: the length changes in the form.
